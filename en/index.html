01Web app · Featured

Caisse Noire

An app to run a sports team's fine kitty. The treasurer hands out fines, everyone can see what they owe, and any dispute is settled before the team's "court".

  • Teams — set up in seconds, invitations with a 6-character code, admin role for the treasurer.
  • Fines & leaderboard — fine schedule set by the treasurer, a "biggest payers" leaderboard and badges.
  • Court — every fine can be contested, and the jury decides.
  • Bookkeeping — payments and expenses tracked to the cent.
  • Installable app — PWA with email or magic-link sign-in, offline viewing and push notifications.
  • React
  • Vite
  • Supabase
  • PWA
  • Vercel
